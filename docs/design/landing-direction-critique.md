# Impeccable critique: landing direction "Yantra"

Reviewer pass on `docs/design/landing-direction.md`, judged as a **Persuade** surface for the mass Indian public against PRODUCT.md, references/TASTE.md and both CLAUDE.md files. This is a direction review, so there are no renders yet; the finish review audits the build later.

**Verdict: approve, with 6 fixes before the build.** The world comes from Axiom's own material: an instrument that reads the market season (the Jantar Mantar scale), regime as monsoon season, evidence grades as railway reservation statuses. It avoids all three hates, borrows no skin from the likes, and the Indian faces (Martel, Hind) are off every overused list. The railway grades are the one idea a competitor can't copy-paste, and the public already knows how to read them.

## Must fix

1. **The hero names a real hedge instrument.** `HEDGE DRAFT · NIFTY PUT SPREAD` shown to the mass public reads as a recommendation even when labelled illustrative (PRODUCT.md: no advice wording; SEBI exposure). → `HEDGE DRAFT · INDEX PROTECTION · ILLUSTRATIVE`, with the structure named only on the evidence card in section 05.
2. **Sections 03 and 04 are written for the desk, not the public.** "Up to eight synced charts" and "one arbitrage-free volatility surface" fail the first-time-visitor test. The direction promises "one screen, two depths", so every section needs both: a plain Hind line for the newcomer plus a Martian Mono caption for the desk. For example, 04: "Every options price comes from one consistent model, so the numbers agree everywhere." with caption `ONE ARBITRAGE-FREE SURFACE · SSVI`.
3. **The problem line in 02 is ambiguous.** "By the time the index says bear, the basket is already there" can be read either way. → "Equity-only tools go quiet when the season turns. The warning arrives after the fall."
4. **The public visitor has no honest action.** "See how it reads a market" only scrolls, and "Talk to the desk" is for brokers. V1 has no public sign-up, so the page needs a truthful public action, or it should say plainly that access comes through brokers. **This is a decision for the user**; see the Open question below.
5. **Calibration check.** Warm basalt with one brass signal sits near impeccable's "near-black with one luminous accent" cluster. What keeps it out is the second voice: geru and monsoon as real state colours, the stamp outlines, and pink paper in light mode. Keep all three. If the build drifts to brass alone, it falls into the cluster.
6. **"options overlay" in the basket ring** isn't an asset class an Indian resident holds, and it muddles the diversification story. → Six arcs (large-cap, mid/small-cap, gilts, gold, cash, international via LRS).

## Keep, and protect at build

- The poster is the LCP element and never unmounts, and three.js only loads when gated (WebGL2, no reduced-motion, deviceMemory, saveData). This is the right answer to the risk of a phone heating up.
- Numbers cut and never count up. No hover lift. A 4px radius ceiling. States always carry a word or glyph, not only colour.
- `ILLUSTRATIVE` stays in the caption rail at every frame.
- The footer line "Research tool. Not investment advice. No performance is promised."

## Risks carried

- A pinned 300vh scroll on a phone can feel like it traps the visitor. Cap the pin at 200vh on screens under 768px, and show the section index so they can skip it.
- Pink paper may read as a newspaper. Test it with brokers, as the doc says.
- Every library API in the doc is UNVERIFIED until installed.

## Open question for the user

What does a member of the public do at the end of the page? Options: (a) "Ask your broker for Axiom" with no form; (b) a notify-me email list (needs a privacy notice and storage); (c) no public action, only "Talk to the desk" for brokers.
